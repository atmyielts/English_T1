# Uzbekistan national English exam ("Multilevel"): verified format

Researched 2026-10-24 for the Schood English exam track (`/exam`). The exam is the
CEFR-based multilevel foreign-language test (B1, B2, C1 in one sitting) run by the
Knowledge Assessment Agency (Bilim va malakalarni baholash agentligi, uzbmb.uz,
which now redirects to gov.uz/oz/uzbmb). It replaced single-level exams in 2022
(Cabinet of Ministers decree No. 73, 16 Feb 2022, as cited in source 1).

Rule for the product: **only the facts marked VERIFIED drive scoring and timers.**
Everything else is "practice in the style of" and is labelled that way in the app.

## Scoring (VERIFIED, sources 1, 2)

- Each of the four sections (listening, reading, writing, speaking) is reported on a **0-75** scale.
  75 is the top because the test has no C2 tasks.
- The CEFR result comes from the **average of the four section scores**:

  | Level | Average score |
  |---|---|
  | C1 | 65-75 |
  | B2 | 51-64 |
  | B1 | 38-50 |
  | Below B1 | 0-37 (source 2 prints 1-37 in the 2026 copy) |

- Listening and reading are scored with the Rasch model and standardised (T = 10Z + 50,
  capped at 75). Each listening and reading form has **35 items** (source 1, table 2).
  The Agency publishes only an *approximate* number of correct answers per level (source 2):
  C1 28-35, B2 18-27, B1 10-17, below B1 0-9. Real scores depend on item difficulty, so any
  raw-to-score conversion outside the Agency is an estimate.
- Writing and speaking are marked by human raters against criteria; if the four sections do
  not correlate, writing and speaking are re-marked by another expert (source 2).
- Rounding of the average is not stated in the sources. **ASSUMED:** the app rounds to the
  nearest whole point.

## Listening (partly verified)

- VERIFIED: 35 items; Rasch-scored; split into smaller sections ("testlets"), each aimed at a
  CEFR level (sources 1, 3).
- UNVERIFIED (U1): 6 parts; each recording heard twice; about 35-45 minutes including answer
  transfer. Sources disagree on timing.
- App: "practice in the style of": 4 parts rising from B1 to C1, 24 items, browser
  text-to-speech, each recording may be played up to twice (our rule).

## Reading (partly verified)

- VERIFIED: 35 items; testlets aimed at different CEFR levels; several item types; two
  testlets use gap-filling; one is an **intra-text gap fill** (about 150-word B1 text, 6 gaps,
  each missing content word appears elsewhere in the text, no synonyms accepted) (sources 1, 3).
- UNVERIFIED (U1): 5 parts in 60 minutes; types include multiple choice, summary completion,
  matching information, matching headings.
- App: 4 parts (intra-text gap fill B1, multiple choice B1-B2, matching headings B2, multiple
  choice C1), 24 items, 40-minute practice timer (our proportional choice).

## Writing (VERIFIED, new format from October 2025, sources 7, 8)

| Part | Level | Task | Length | Score |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1.1 | B1 | Informal email to a friend, replying to a short (50-80 word) transactional email that contains 3 action points | about 50 words | 0-5 |
| 1.2 | B2 | Formal email on the same situation to a named reader (manager, customer service...) | 120-150 words | 0-5 |
| 2 | C1 | Online text (blog post, forum post or magazine article) on a topic of general interest, prompt of 30-50 words | 180-200 words | 0-6 |

- Total time **60 minutes**, no fixed split; about 25 minutes for Part 1 and 35 for Part 2 recommended.
- Holistic, task-specific scales. Descriptors cover: task requirements covered, style/register,
  grammar range and accuracy, punctuation and spelling, vocabulary, cohesion/linking.
  Length notes in the descriptors: Task 1.2 at 25-60 words fits band 2, 24 words or fewer band 1;
  Part 2 at 38-90 words fits band 2, 37 or fewer band 1; Task 1.1 at half the length or less fits
  band 2. Off-topic, memorised, copied, mostly first-language or blank answers score 0.
- Expert score 0-16 converts to 0-75 with the official table (16→75, 15→69, 14→65, 13→63,
  12→61, 11→57, 10→53, 9→50, 8→47, 7→43, 6→40, 5→37, 4→33, 3→28, 2→21, 1→14, 0.5→10, 0→0;
  half points in between), implemented in `_shared/english-exam-format.ts`.
- Superseded: the 2023 rules (task 1 = 12 points, task 2 = 24 points, 0-36 table, source 2)
  are kept in the doc only for history.

## Speaking (VERIFIED, new format from September 2024, sources 4, 5, 6)

| Part | Level | Questions | Task | Time | Prep |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1.1 | A1-A2 | 3 (Q1-3) | Personal questions on familiar topics | 30 s each | none |
| 1.2 | B1 | 3 (Q4-6) | Two pictures on one topic: describe/compare, give and justify opinions | Q4 45 s; Q5-6 30 s | none |
| 2 | B2 | 1 (Q7, three questions shown together with a picture) | Abstract topic, advantages and disadvantages; no need to describe the picture | 2 min | 1 min |
| 3 | C1 | 1 (Q8) | A statement with FOR and AGAINST points; choose points from each side and give a balanced argument | 2 min | 1 min |

- Rating scale: Q1-3 group 0-5, Q4-6 group 0-5, Q7 0-5, Q8 0-6 (raw total 0-21). Descriptors
  cover topic relevance (how many answers are on topic), grammar, vocabulary, pronunciation,
  fluency (pausing, reformulation) and cohesion.
- Raw 0-21 converts to 0-75 with the official table (21→75, 20→71, 19→67, 18→64, 17→61,
  16→57, 15→54, 14→51, 13→49, 12→46, 11→43, 10→40, 9→38, 8→35, 7→32, 6→29, 5→26, 4→23,
  3→19, 2→15, 1→11, 0.5→10, 0→0; half points in between).
- App limits: browser speech recognition (or typing) gives a transcript only, so pronunciation
  cannot really be judged; results say so. Pictures are replaced by short written scene
  descriptions (we have no licensed photos), labelled as such.

## Not used in the app

- Fees and exam dates: no current official fee/date page could be confirmed. The Agency's
  scientific centre advertised paid computer-based diagnostic mocks (e.g. 119,000 UZS on
  23 Dec 2025, source 9) but that is not the exam fee. The learner enters their own exam date.
- The Agency's official sample papers (zip on https://uzbmb.uz/page/test_namunalari) were not
  opened or copied. All practice content is original.
- Conflicting unofficial claims seen and rejected: "30 points per section, 120 total"; speaking
  "4 criteria, no pronunciation"; old 150/250-word writing tasks.
