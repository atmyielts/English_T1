## Sequence

```
PE-01 language packs ──► V-RU Russian ──► V-KO Korean ──► V-LANG German/Turkish
PE-02 exam-format registry ─┬─► V-ADM Math ──► V-ADM other subjects
PE-03 subject engine ───────┘        └──► V-SEL selective schools (reuses math/logic)
(no new engine) ──► V-ABR study-abroad bundle   (packaging + counselling)
PE-01 + V-RU ──► V-TCH teacher certification     (packaging of language content)
```

1. **First**, build `PE-01` and launch `V-RU`. It's the cheapest proof that the platform is multi-language. A language pack, not an app.
2. **In parallel**, validate `V-ADM` demand and confirm the current official format. Build `PE-02` and `PE-03`, then launch Math.
3. **Next**, `V-ABR` and `V-TCH`. These are mostly packaging, so revenue comes quickly.
4. **Then** `V-KO`, `V-SEL` and `V-LANG`, in the order the validation data supports.

## Validation gate before any vertical moves past `idea`

A vertical moves to `validated` only with recorded evidence of at least two of these:
- 100 or more waitlist sign-ups from a landing page or Telegram post, at a stated price.
- 5 or more centers say they would pay, with a named price range.
- Search or Telegram demand data, with the source and date recorded in the vertical doc.
- A paid pre-sale or a paid pilot cohort.

## Deliberately skipped (and why)

| Idea | Why not now |
|---|---|
| General programming or IT courses | Crowded, free state options, and weak fit with an exam-and-mastery engine |
| Everyday K-12 homework help across all subjects | Low price, very high content cost, unclear outcome to sell |
| GMAT, GRE, A-level, IB | Tiny local volume. Revisit through V-ABR if asked |
| Building our own video course library | High cost, low differentiation. Our edge is practice, feedback and prediction |
| Live tutoring as a core product | Operations-heavy. The existing `teacher-marketplace` can stay a side channel |

## Portfolio-level metrics (monthly)

- Revenue and gross margin per vertical (after AI and content cost).
- Number of verticals with a positive contribution margin.
- Cross-sell: % of learners active in 2 or more verticals.
- B2B: centers using content from 2 or more verticals through Assessment Cloud.
