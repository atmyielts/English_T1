# Scoped Academy finance, payroll and paid vacation

This candidate closes a financial correctness and authority gap. A cashier in
branch A and admin in branch B keeps those capabilities independent. Both
financial workspaces use fresh database authority, institution-bound references,
and branch filtering before list limits and per-currency aggregation. Shared
institution account credit is explicitly distinguished from branch revenue.

Twenty-one service-only financial commands commit current authority, business
state, audit and immutable retry receipts together. An uncertain browser action
retains its exact values, actor and key across reload. A second action is blocked
until the captured request is confirmed. Monetary inputs and responses preserve
whole units beyond JavaScript safe integers. Cash shifts refuse mixed currencies,
duplicate linked receipts and unavailable cash; card/Click book entries do not
change physical closing cash. Internal entries are not external payment proof.

Percentage compensation uses net allocations of paid tuition, dated in the
classroom branch timezone. Payroll includes real delivered lessons and active
students, supports base plus student rules, retains source IDs and immutable
versioned snapshots, and refuses overlapping periods or recalculation of paid
payroll. Academy-wide rules require an academy-wide period to prevent a fixed
salary being paid once per branch. Hourly classroom components reconcile exactly
with the rounded period amount. Existing historical snapshots remain intact.

Vacation starts on the current branch-local date. Manual resume carries forward
elapsed paused service days on dated subscriptions and records the preserved
days. Replay cannot extend the end date twice. This does not activate automatic
wallet exhaustion, future scheduled pauses, or any provider notification.

Qualification exercises all 21 commands, exact money, approval and payout replay,
cash reconciliation, failed-receipt rollback, mixed grants, scoped reads, full
totals, vacation, global rules and hourly rounding. Native contention includes
expiry while receipt insertion waits behind a SHARE lock; both business state
and receipt must roll back. Browser coverage includes a real reload of the pending
request and phone rendering of exact per-currency totals.

Release through protected exact-head CI and tracked Pages, followed by exactly
four reviewed additive migrations above the 221 installed baseline and the
source-preserving Academy bundle. The v86 capture contains 29 runtime modules;
28 remain unchanged, the owned index is patched and one financial adapter is
added. There are 30 prepared runtime modules. No unrelated payment, assessment or
proctor migration/source is part of this activation.

Rollback keeps immutable financial evidence and additive schema. Use a tracked
Pages rollback or an owned source-preserving forward repair. Hosted acceptance,
cleanup and the remaining twelve-row/all-service work continue after activation;
this candidate is not a declaration that every closeout item is finished.
